## Objective

Find the first host address, last host address, broadcast address, and the subnet mask for a given, randomized IP address.

### 255.95.252.216/30

First, I checked how many hosts are in a /30 as I didn't remember. I used the formula below to do so.

```md
32(Total bits)-CIDR = 2*left over bits = hosts
```

To find useable hosts, you would subtract your answer by two.
