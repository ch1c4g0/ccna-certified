
# What is Subnetting?

Subnetting is the act of breaking a network into smaller, more manageable pieces. This is especially important in larger environments.

By the end, you should be able to answer the questions below.

1. What Subnet a host is on
2. Subnet this network into at least 10 Subnets
3. Subnet this network into Subnets each having 8 hosts in them.
